# Release brief

RL Field Guide is a bilingual, reading-first web guide for adults who can do basic arithmetic but do not yet know reinforcement learning, probability notation, calculus, neural networks, or LLM post-training.

It has 16 ordered lessons and seven learning stages. Every lesson is readable without a click and follows the same cognitive order:

1. a concrete situation;
2. a semantic HTML/CSS knowledge diagram;
3. a complete numeric example;
4. plain-language concept names;
5. equations only when useful;
6. a visible boundary and transfer example.

The site has no quiz, hidden answer, lesson completion score, progress tracker, account, backend, payment, or certification claim. Language links, lesson links, and the reading-theme switch remain ordinary navigation controls.
